## Listing text

App name: **Cluewoven**

Apple subtitle: **Detective logic puzzles**

Google short description: **Solve 100 detective puzzles. Follow the clues, place suspects, find the culprit.**

Full description (both stores):

Every witness tells the truth. Even the murderer.

Cluewoven is a collection of 100 detective logic puzzles. Read the testimony, place each suspect and the victim on the floor plan, and work out who was alone with the victim.

Each person appears once. No two people share a row or column. Furniture blocks a square. Combine room alibis, nearby objects, and the relationships between witnesses to reconstruct the scene.

Start with a guided practice mystery, then explore ten chapters across five difficulty levels. Work your way from compact 6×6 scenes to intricate 9×9 investigations. Every case is available from the beginning.

• 100 mysteries with one validated solution each
• Progressive hints: evidence first, explanation next, position when you need it
• Pencil marks, undo, and a notebook for reviewed testimony
• Automatic device-local saves and a timer that pauses when you leave the investigation
• Chapter-completion celebrations and spoiler-free result sharing
• Optional sound effects and haptic feedback
• Offline play, with no account required

A little logic. A little mystery.

## Review notes

No login or backend is required. All 100 cases are available in Case files. Help opens the rules guide and a replayable practice scene. Settings → About & support includes the privacy policy and support email. Email and result-sharing actions open system interfaces only when requested by the player. Nothing is sent automatically.

To verify a solved state naturally, use the three hint stages and place the revealed person, repeating until the scene is complete. Choose Solve case and accuse the sole suspect in the victim's room. The result screen offers a spoiler-free sharing preview. Do not upload internal puzzle solutions as public screenshots.
